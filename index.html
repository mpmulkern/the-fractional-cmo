FAQ

Questions, answered straight

What is The Fractional CMO?

15 AI agents for marketing — brand positioning playbook, competitive landscape, category narrative, PR strategy, content calendar, campaign briefs, social plan, GTM launch playbook, SEO, paid media, demand gen, product marketing, marketing scorecard, attribution model, and board marketing deck. Each is QC-reviewed and scored against a 100-point rubric.

How does it work, and what do I need?

You bring your own Anthropic (Claude) API key, fill out a short intake about your situation, and the agent drafts the deliverable, checks it, and scores it against a 100-point rubric — then you review and ship. All you need is an Anthropic key, about two minutes to set up the first time.

What does it cost?

A single agent is $1,200/year ($600 for 3 months, $900 for 6). A 3–4 agent bundle is $3,500/year, and the full CMO Suite (all 15 agents, 5 seats) is $12,000/year. On top of that you pay your own Anthropic usage — usually a few cents to a couple of dollars per deliverable, with no markup from us.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Everything runs on your own Anthropic key. Your data passes through our gateway only in the moment to call Claude — it is never written to disk, logged, or stored. We keep only your license details and basic usage counts, and we never sell or share your data.

How accurate is it, and is this professional advice?

Treat every deliverable as an expert-quality first draft, not a final plan of record. It is rubric-scored, but validate the assumptions and have your marketing leaders review before acting. It is not professional advice.

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. You can see a free live sample deliverable — no key and no signup — so you can judge the output first. Licenses also come with a 14-day guarantee: if your first deliverable is not board-ready, email us within 14 days for a full refund of the license fee.

Can I tailor it to my company and workflow?

Every deliverable is shaped by your intake — your brand, audience, channels, and goals — and you can re-run as many times as you like to refine it. Suite owners can also have a custom agent built for their exact workflow.

Do I have to connect it to HubSpot or my CRM?

No — by design. You paste the data a deliverable needs; nothing connects to your marketing stack, which keeps your data private.
